How to play

The round

Every round splits the room into hiders and hunters. Hiders get a prep window to find a spot, paint their body to match it and lock a pose. Then the hunters are released with paint guns. Hiders win if even one of them is still unfound when the clock runs out.

Hiding well

  • Position first, paint second. Find a surface with clutter, shadow or pattern — a flat wall shows your outline no matter how good the colour is.
  • Break the silhouette. Crouch, lie flat, curl up, cling to a wall. A person-shape is what gets spotted, not a hue.
  • Use the eyedropper. The nearby-surfaces palette samples what is actually around you. Auto-Mimic does the whole body in one press.
  • Then stop moving. Motion is louder than colour.

Hunting well

  • Sweep a consistent route instead of wandering — you are looking for what changed.
  • Compare shapes, not colours. Look for a silhouette that does not belong.
  • Sonar Ping reveals anything with line of sight inside its radius. Save it for a room you cannot read.
  • Shoot anything suspicious. Paint is free; time is not.

Controls

Playing with friends

Create a room and send the code or the link — anyone who opens it joins straight into your lobby. If they are signed in with a username you can invite them by handle instead, and the invite waits for them if they are offline.

Fair play

The server simulates every player itself and only tells your game about hiders you could actually see, so wallhacks have nothing to draw. Movement, fire rate and every hit are checked server-side. Repeat violations get you removed from the room.
